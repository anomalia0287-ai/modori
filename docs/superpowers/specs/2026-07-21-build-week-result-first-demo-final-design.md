# Build Week Result-First Demo Final Design

Date: 2026-07-21 KST

## Decision and supersession

Produce one English Build Week submission video that runs approximately 2 minutes
44 seconds to 2 minutes 50 seconds and remains safely below the three-minute rule.
This design supersedes the editorial story and fixed 175-second timing in
`2026-07-21-build-week-human-problem-demo-reedit-design.md`. The existing 2:05
candidate remains evidence and source material, but it is not the upload master.

The final story is result-first and human-led:

1. establish the creator as a sociology graduate who will soon begin graduate
   school and wants other students to experience an approachable,
   subscription-free tool;
2. reveal the real result and editable report within the first three seconds;
3. demonstrate one complete question-to-report Guided Mode path on 649 released
   student records;
4. demonstrate a clearly separate causal request that ends in abstention;
5. show concrete Codex and GPT-5.6 engineering contribution through actual
   repository, regression, integration, and verification evidence; and
6. close on researcher control and the product principle that kindness is system
   behavior.

No competitor price, currency conversion, competitor interface, or third-party logo
appears. Cost is expressed once as Modori's positive `subscription-free` property.

## Audience and judging alignment

- **Potential Impact:** a sociology graduate built the tool they wanted other
  students to have, and the video demonstrates that exact beginner-facing path.
- **Design:** a moving, coherent product journey covers import, meaning, research
  task, clarification, recommendation, exact review, Prepare, Confirm, separate Run,
  result, report, and recovery behavior.
- **Quality of the Idea:** Modori starts from the research question, commits variable
  meaning before recommendation, can abstain, and preserves researcher control.
- **Technological Implementation:** one concentrated engineering section shows how
  Codex and GPT-5.6 moved product decisions through executable contracts, red tests,
  implementation, packaged-app verification, semantic integration, and the final
  source suite.

## Evidence and asset boundaries

- Successful product motion, result, and report come from the verified existing raw
  walkthrough and hash-bound DOCX.
- Causal abstention comes from the existing fresh-state capture if its hash and
  privacy review remain valid.
- Engineering montages use only repository paths, exact test names, committed source
  excerpts, integration-ledger values, and saved verification output. They do not
  show a private Codex conversation or invent a UI that was not captured.
- If a recovery behavior lacks suitable product footage, show its exact
  observed-symptom/test/fix/pass chain. Do not stage a fake recovery screen.
- Dataset attribution is textual:
  `Cortez, P. (2008), UCI Machine Learning Repository · CC BY 4.0 · DOI 10.24432/C5TG7T`.
- The report cutaway uses the actual generated DOCX render without application chrome
  or a third-party logo.

## Motion, pacing, audio, and typography contract

- Prefer one locally synthesized Kokoro ONNX English voice at one fixed global
  speed for every spoken cue. Dependency and model downloads may use the network,
  but narration synthesis must run on this computer. Record the package version,
  voice name, model and voice-data hashes, and selected global speed.
- Bound the local-neural-voice trial to installation plus a representative
  pronunciation/duration sample. If installation, licensing, pronunciation,
  decoding, or final-duration gates fail, fall back to the already verified
  Microsoft Zira Desktop SAPI voice at rate `0`. Do not use `edge-tts`: it calls
  Microsoft Edge's online TTS service and therefore is not a local-synthesis
  replacement. Per-cue speech acceleration or deceleration is prohibited in both
  paths.
- Trim only synthesizer trailing silence. Add a measured 0.6-0.8 second transition
  tail after each utterance; reject any detected silence interval over 1.25 seconds.
- Product motion remains at 1.0x. Waiting, native pickers, and irrelevant cursor
  travel are removed by hard cuts. No moving excerpt may exceed 1.25x.
- The opening brand card remains for about 2.5 seconds while narration has already
  begun. The real result must be visible no later than 00:03.
- Important product states remain legible for 4-6 seconds. Engineering evidence
  cards remain legible for 2.5-3.5 seconds and animate through progress, crop, or
  gentle movement rather than sitting as a dead slide.
- No screen is held merely to approach three minutes. Final duration follows the
  measured narration and must land between 164 and 170 seconds.
- Burned-in text is short English copy only, at most two headline lines plus one
  evidence line. Full English narration is delivered in the separate SRT.
- Product values and active controls must never be obscured by overlays.
- Visual system: near-black navy `#06111F`, Royal Blue `#3166B7`, warm white
  `#F7F3EA`, and the existing Modori wordmark treatment.
- No copyrighted music is used. Voice remains clear and consistent at approximately
  `-16 LUFS`, with true peak no higher than `-1.5 dBTP`.

## Claim and compliance boundaries

- The successful result is Spearman `rho = 0.2747118483356099`, two-sided
  `p = 1.060624038270125e-12`, `n = 649`, excluded rows `= 0`. Display may round to
  `rho = 0.275` and `p = 0.000`; narration must say `p below .001`, never literal
  zero.
- The result is an association in the released records, not a causal effect or a
  population estimate.
- `NIST-certified Modori`, `AI-validated statistics`, universal recommendation
  validity, expert equivalence, competitor superiority, and human-validation claims
  are prohibited.
- Codex and GPT-5.6 are described as development collaborators. The video does not
  imply that a generative model performs the released statistical calculation.
- Every current release-facing document, subtitle, and video card uses the single
  final source-suite result `3,393 passed, 5 skipped`. A different historical
  Office-benchmark gate remains scoped to its dated runbook and must not appear in
  the current submission story.
- No competitor price screen, currency conversion, third-party logo, copyrighted
  music, stock footage, private identity, private path, notification, API key,
  account name, or unrelated application appears.
- The successful request and causal abstention are visibly separate tasks.

## Acceptance gates

1. The final MP4 is under 180 seconds and between 164 and 170 seconds after measured
   audio-derived editing; no duration padding is permitted.
2. Result is visible by 00:03, and every required story beat appears in the order
   above.
3. All narration cues use one recorded voice engine, voice name, and fixed global
   speed or rate. Full-media silence detection finds no interval longer than 1.25
   seconds.
4. Every moving Modori excerpt is 1.0x unless a recorded exception at or below 1.25x
   is necessary; the segment audit records the exact maximum.
5. H.264 High-compatible video is 1,920 × 1,080, square pixels, constant 30 fps, with
   AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo audio and fast-start MP4 layout.
6. Full audio/video decode exits zero. Frame count, duration, loudness, true peak,
   black-frame scan, and subtitle endpoint are recorded.
7. Opening and later result shots and the report agree on `rho = 0.275`, displayed
   `p = 0.000`, `n = 649`, and excluded rows `= 0`.
8. Import preview, full table, Variable Meaning Gate, bounded questions, candidate,
   exact configuration, Prepare, Confirm, empty result, separate Run, result,
   editable report, and separate causal abstention are all visibly identifiable.
9. Engineering evidence accurately shows the three authority boundaries, three
   real-UI fix chains, Royal Blue integration ledger, and exact source-suite result.
10. Original-resolution frame review finds no crop, blocked control, unreadable key
    value, private information, or prohibited third-party price/logo screen.
11. The exact MP4 and SRT hashes, media probes, source asset hashes, and remaining
    manual-review status are recorded before the file is described as upload-ready.
12. Only one clearly named MP4 and one matching SRT are handed to the user for final
    playback and upload; superseded candidates are not presented as choices.
