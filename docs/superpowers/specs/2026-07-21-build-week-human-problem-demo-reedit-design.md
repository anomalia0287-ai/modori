# Build Week Human-Problem Demo Re-edit Design

Date: 2026-07-21 KST

## Decision

Re-edit the existing 175-second Build Week candidate around one memorable product
claim: **Modori asks before it calculates, abstains when the supported evidence is
insufficient, and waits for an explicit Run action.** Preserve the verified
result-first opening and real UCI dataset, but replace the idle entry-screen Codex
overlay and uneven speed ramps with a deliberate dark opening card, natural-speed
product actions joined by hard cuts, one clearly separate causal-abstention vignette,
and a short repository-evidence montage showing how Codex with GPT-5.6 contributed.

The creator may be described publicly as a social-science student. The video may say
in first person that the creator built the statistical workflow they needed. It must
not expose the creator's name, school, account name, private paths, photograph, or
other unnecessary personal information.

## Why this structure

The current candidate proves a complete workflow but mixes near-real-time footage
with compressions as high as 5.2x and leaves the Modori entry screen visually idle
for 16 seconds under dense contribution overlays. The viewer experiences the edit
rather than the product.

Anthropic's official retrospective on its Built with Opus 4.6 hackathon states that
four of five winners were not professional developers and that the winners applied
domain knowledge to housing, healthcare, infrastructure, music, and education. That
supports a concise first-person domain story, but it does not justify copying another
event's exact video grammar or replacing Modori's stronger observed-data evidence
with synthetic data.

## Frozen editorial boundaries

- Runtime is exactly 175.00 seconds and therefore below the three-minute limit.
- Broadcast language is English voiceover. A complete English SRT is delivered as a
  separate YouTube caption file.
- Burned-in copy is limited to short, high-contrast English phrases; the final MP4
  does not burn the complete narration over the product UI.
- A Korean operator translation remains in `docs/build-week/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`; it is
  not shown in the submitted video.
- No third-party price page, third-party logo, copyrighted music, stock footage,
  mock product screen, synthetic dataset, or unverified benchmark claim appears.
- The successful analysis uses the existing 649-row UCI Student Performance
  Portuguese-course table and the already verified Spearman result.
- The causal abstention is explicitly introduced as a second request. It is not
  edited to look like part of the successful noncausal task.
- Product actions run at 1.0x whenever possible. Any continuous speed adjustment is
  capped at 1.25x. Waiting is removed with hard cuts, not extreme time warps.
- Every product state is captured from the exact packaged launcher bound in the
  current Build Week evidence packet.
- Codex contribution is framed positively and concretely. It names specification,
  failing tests, implementation, Windows verification, the Variable Meaning Gate,
  ledger authority, separate Run, Excel recovery, safe Word export, and failure
  recovery. It does not imply that a generative model runs inside Modori.
- `NIST-certified Modori`, `NIST-certified references`, `AI-validated statistics`,
  SPSS superiority, causal effect, population generalization, and automatic expert
  equivalence are prohibited phrases.
- The numerical evidence caption is exactly:
  `3,393 passed · NIST StRD checks · R cross-engine anchors`.

## Motion and typography contract

- Opening and closing cards use near-black navy `#06111F`, Royal Blue `#3166B7`,
  warm white `#F7F3EA`, and the existing Modori wordmark treatment.
- No card contains more than two headline lines plus one small evidence line.
- Product overlays use at most seven words per visible line and never cover the
  active control or the value being discussed.
- Each important product state remains fully legible for at least 2.5 seconds.
- Cursor travel and native-picker waiting are cut away. Click-to-state transitions
  remain visible at natural speed.
- The Codex montage uses only repository filenames, test names, commit-safe source
  excerpts, and saved verification results. It contains no private session text.
- The Word report uses the already demonstrated DOCX render; no mock report is made.

## Acceptance gates

1. Exact duration `175.000` seconds, 5,250 frames at constant 30 fps.
2. H.264 High, 1,920 × 1,080, square pixels, AAC 48 kHz stereo, fast-start MP4.
3. Full decode exits zero; no black interval longer than 0.25 seconds outside the
   intentional branded opening and closing cards.
4. Integrated loudness target `-16 LUFS`, true peak no higher than `-1.5 dBTP`.
5. No continuous source-to-output compression exceeds 1.25x.
6. The successful and abstention paths are visibly identified as separate requests.
7. The full-width Modori product frame is preserved; no left or right control is
   cropped.
8. Opening result, later result, and Word report match `rho = 0.275`, displayed
   `p = 0.000`, `n = 649`, and `excluded = 0`.
9. The video visibly demonstrates active product movement, not a slideshow standing
   in for the complete workflow.
10. The submitted MP4 contains only short burned-in English labels. The separate
    English SRT covers the full voiceover and ends at exactly `00:02:55,000`.
11. Frame sampling and OCR/privacy review find no user name, school, private path,
    account identifier, notification, unrelated application, or third-party price
    screen/logo.
12. The final SHA-256, file size, codec probe, frame count, audio measurements,
    black-frame scan, speed audit, subtitle hash, and manual visual review are recorded
    in `docs/build-week/VIDEO_VERIFICATION.md` before upload.
